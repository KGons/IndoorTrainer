📱 Run on Phone Without PC

Direct Phone Bluetooth: The app connects directly from your phone's Bluetooth chip to your smart trainer (Elite Direto, Kickr, Tacx, etc.). Once installed, your PC is never needed!

Step 1: Install to Phone Home Screen

On Android Chrome / Edge: Tap the 📲 Install button in the header, or tap the browser menu (⋮) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

The app now installs as a native standalone app with its own icon and opens full-screen without address bars!

Step 2: 100% Offline Capability

Thanks to the built-in Service Worker, this app works completely offline without internet or Wi-Fi. You can ride in your garage, basement, or pain cave anytime.

Step 3: Free Permanent Cloud Hosting (GitHub Pages)

To access this app URL on your phone anytime from anywhere:

  1. Create a free GitHub repository and push this folder.
  2. In GitHub repo Settings → Pages, set branch to main and save.
  3. Open the provided https://... link on your Android phone and install it once.

Handlebar Cockpit Features

  • Audio Beeps: 3-2-1 countdown sounds at interval ends.
  • Haptic Buzz: Vibrates on countdown and interval switches.
  • Screen Awake: Prevents phone screen from dimming/sleeping.
  • Landscape Cockpit: Turn phone sideways on handlebars for widescreen cockpit mode!
